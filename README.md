 # 🤖 ClarityOS

**ClarityOS** is an **AI-powered Multi-Agent Enterprise Intelligence Platform** designed to transform meeting discussions and business data into actionable insights.

The platform combines **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **multi-agent analysis**, and **business intelligence techniques** to automatically generate meeting summaries, extract tasks, validate claims against uploaded datasets, assess risks, generate executive briefings, and export professional reports.

This project demonstrates the practical application of **Generative AI, Multi-Agent Systems, Prompt Engineering, Data Analytics, and Business Intelligence** within a single enterprise-focused solution.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Organizations often struggle with:

- Long meeting transcripts
- Missed action items
- Unverified assumptions
- Disconnected business data
- Time-consuming report preparation

ClarityOS solves these challenges by combining meeting intelligence and business data analysis into a unified AI platform.

The application enables users to upload:

- Meeting transcripts (.txt)
- Business datasets (.csv)

and automatically generates:

- Meeting summaries
- Task extraction
- Data insights
- Claim validation
- Confidence scoring
- Risk assessment
- Executive briefings
- PDF reports

---

# 🛠️ Project Workflow

## 1️⃣ Meeting Intelligence Agent

Analyzes uploaded meeting transcripts and generates:

- Key discussion points
- Concerns raised
- Decisions made
- Executive-level summaries

This helps stakeholders quickly understand important meeting outcomes.

---

## 2️⃣ Task Extraction Agent

Identifies explicit action items discussed during meetings.

Extracts:

- Task description
- Owner/Assignee
- Deadline
- Priority level
- Confidence level

The agent avoids creating tasks from assumptions and focuses only on explicit actions.

---

## 3️⃣ Data Intelligence Agent

Analyzes uploaded business datasets and generates:

- Dataset overview
- Statistical insights
- Regional performance metrics
- Sales and profit summaries
- Missing value analysis

Provides factual evidence directly from business data.

---

## 4️⃣ Correlation Analysis Agent

Compares meeting discussions against available business data.

Classifies findings into:

- Supported Claims
- Unsupported Claims
- Not Verifiable Claims
- Additional Data Findings

This prevents unsupported business assumptions from appearing in reports.

---

## 5️⃣ Confidence Scoring Agent

Assigns confidence levels to business claims.

Examples:

- 100% → Fully supported by data
- 0% → No evidence available

Provides transparency regarding decision-making confidence.

---

## 6️⃣ Risk Assessment Agent

Evaluates business and operational risks using:

- Meeting evidence
- Dataset evidence

Generates:

- Risk descriptions
- Severity levels
- Evidence-based reasoning
- Recommended actions

Unverifiable risks are clearly marked as:

**Severity: Not Verifiable**

---

## 7️⃣ Executive Intelligence Agent

Generates concise executive-level reports containing:

- Situation Overview
- Verified Findings
- Key Risks
- Immediate Actions
- Recommendations

Designed specifically for business leaders and decision-makers.

---

## 8️⃣ Report Generation

Creates downloadable reports including:

- Meeting Summary
- Tasks
- Data Insights
- Correlation Analysis
- Confidence Scores
- Risk Assessment
- Executive Briefing

Reports can be exported as professional PDF documents.

# 🎯 Why This Project Matters

Businesses generate large amounts of meeting discussions and operational data every day, but much of that information remains underutilized.

ClarityOS demonstrates how **Generative AI and Multi-Agent Systems can convert unstructured discussions and structured business data into actionable intelligence**, enabling faster decisions, better risk awareness, and improved operational visibility.

The project showcases a practical enterprise use case of AI beyond simple chatbots by combining:

- Meeting Intelligence
- Business Analytics
- Risk Assessment
- Executive Reporting
- Evidence Validation

into a single intelligent decision-support platform.
